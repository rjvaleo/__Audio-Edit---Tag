Grain control glyphs

One drawing language for every control in MultiGrainLive's grain engine, in the spirit of Teenage Engineering's panels: a glyph instead of a label, drawn on the same small grid, and redrawn live as the control moves. Move any slider to see its picture change.

Proposal, not yet in the app. In the app each glyph would sit above its knob or slider and redraw as you turn it. The words stay in the hover help and for screen readers.
