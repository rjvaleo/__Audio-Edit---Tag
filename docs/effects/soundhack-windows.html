SoundHack 0.896 — every dialog, at 1:1

Redrawn from the binary's own DLOG, DITL, CNTL and MENU resources: each window's size and title, and every item's rectangle, kind and text, exactly. Yellow numbers are item numbers. A pop-up shows its first choice, and every pop-up's full list is under its window. The Function Window's preset icons are PICTs, shown as dotted boxes.
