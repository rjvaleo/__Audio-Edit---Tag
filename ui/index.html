Waveform
Neon, so the sound reads against the surface behind it. The grain marks keep their own colour.
Direction is read, not chosen: a palette that averages light becomes a light theme, one that averages dark becomes a dark one. Every colour on the card counts — they are sorted dark to light and spread along the interface's own lightness axis, so the dark ones become the surfaces and the light ones become the text. Lightness itself comes from a fixed ladder, which is what keeps text legible whatever the palette holds.
Palette
A palette gives colour and direction; the ladder of surfaces, text and borders is derived from it, so contrast holds whatever the colours are. Good, warning and bad keep their own hues — a theme is not allowed to repaint what they mean.
